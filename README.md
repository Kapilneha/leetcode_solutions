# LeetCode Solutions

This repo is where I track my journey of learning data structures and algorithms.

I'm still early in this process, and that's okay. Every problem here represents something I sat with, struggled through, and eventually figured out (or learned from when I didn't).

## How I work through problems
- Give myself 15–20 minutes to think before looking anything up
- Write a short note on my approach and what I learned, even when the solution isn't perfect
- Come back to old problems later and try solving them again, without peeking

## Why this repo exists
Not to look impressive — to actually build the skill, and have something honest to show for the practice along the way.
